# Research: Visual Polish and Parity (Phase 13.1.1)

This document outlines the technical approach for achieving 1:1 design parity with the Amber Brand mockups.

## 1. ArticleCard Spectrum Footer
The design shows a 2-4dp horizontal spectrum bar at the bottom of the card.
- **Implementation**: Instead of a full `BiasHeatmap`, we will use a `Box` with `fillMaxWidth`, `height(4.dp)`, and `background(ProjectTheme.bias.gradient)`.
- **Action Area**: The "+ Track" action should be a `Text` or `TextButton` placed in a `Row` above or adjacent to the spectrum bar.

## 2. Typography & Color Weights
The "Premium" feel relies on specific color usage:
- **Source Names**: Use `Amber300` (Dark) or `Amber600` (Light) for high readability. Currently, some areas use the primary `Amber500` which may have lower contrast on white.
- **FontWeight**: Use `FontWeight.Bold` for "Read original story" and `FontWeight.Medium` for source names to match the "Inter" weights in the mockup.
- **Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono` is verified as available in `Type.kt` and should be used for data labels (timestamps, unrated counts).

## 3. High-Fidelity App Icon Gradients
Android `VectorDrawable` gradients are the bottleneck for many icon refreshes.
- **Syntax**:
  ```xml
  <path ...>
      <aapt:attr name="android:fillColor">
          <gradient
              android:startX="0" android:startY="0"
              android:endX="108" android:endY="108"
              android:type="linear">
              <item android:offset="0" android:color="#FCD34D" /> <!-- Amber300 -->
              <item android:offset="1" android:color="#D97706" /> <!-- Amber600 -->
          </gradient>
      </aapt:attr>
  </path>
  ```
- **Capability Check**: Verified that the project target is high enough to support these aapt attributes natively.

## 4. Comparison Perspective Groups
The mockups show color-coded headers and dividers that "anchor" each perspective section.
- **Implementation**: Update `PerspectiveHeader` to accept a `Color` parameter and apply it to:
  - The title text.
  - The badge container.
  - A horizontal divider with `alpha = 0.2f`.
- **Matched Article Accent**: Ensure the 3px left border in `MatchedArticleCard` uses the exact perspective color.
