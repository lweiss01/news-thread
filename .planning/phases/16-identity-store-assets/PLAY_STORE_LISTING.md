### 3. Long Description (Finalized)

**Stop hunting for updates. Start tracking the thread.**

NewsThread is more than a news aggregator—it’s the first app that lets you follow the evolution of a story as it happens. While others give you headlines, NewsThread gives you the thread.

**TRACK THE EVOLUTION**
Found a story that matters? Don't wait for the next big news cycle. Hit 'Track' and NewsThread’s local AI starts working for you. You’ll be proactively notified the moment a materially new update is published or when a significant new perspective emerges—ensuring you never lose the thread of a developing story.

**BEYOND THE HEADLINE**
Track a story across multiple sources and the entire political spectrum. Our unique Bias Heatmap visualizes how different outlets report the same event, allowing you to compare left, center, and right perspectives in one place.

**MEDIA LITERACY, BUILT-IN**
Break out of your echo chamber. NewsThread doesn't just show you what happened; it shows you how the narrative is being shaped. 

**PRIVACY-FIRST & ON-DEVICE**
Your curiosity is your business. NewsThread performs all bias analysis and story matching locally on your device. No accounts, no cloud tracking, and a specialized offline mode so you stay informed anywhere.
