## Full Description (4000 char max)

```
Stop reading one headline. Start following the whole story.

NewsThread shows you how every news source covers the same event — plotted across the political bias spectrum from Left to Center to Right. See the full picture, not just one perspective.

📊 BIAS SPECTRUM
Every story comes with a visual bias heatmap showing exactly where each source falls on the political spectrum. Compare coverage from outlets like the New York Times, Fox News, AP, BBC, and hundreds more — all in one view.

🔍 COMPARE PERSPECTIVES
Tap any article to instantly see how other sources report the same story. Articles are grouped by Left, Center, and Right perspectives so you can compare framing, emphasis, and what each side leaves out.

📌 TRACK DEVELOPING STORIES
Found a story that matters? Hit Track and get notified the moment new coverage appears. Watch stories evolve across sources with update counts, new article badges, and a full coverage timeline.

📰 STORY ANALYSIS
Dive deep into any tracked story to see the full breakdown: how many sources are covering it, the bias distribution across the spectrum, and every article organized by political perspective.

✅ SOURCE RELIABILITY RATINGS
Every source is rated for reliability using data from AllSides, Ad Fontes Media, and Media Bias/Fact Check. Green means highly reliable. Orange means proceed with caution. You always know what you're reading.

🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST, ON-DEVICE
Your reading habits are your business. NewsThread performs all bias analysis and story matching locally on your device. No accounts required. No cloud tracking. No ads. No data collection.

⚡ FAST, CLEAN FEED
A modern dark interface designed for reading. Rich article cards with source names, timestamps, reliability badges, images, and bias indicators — all at a glance. Pull to refresh for the latest stories.

---

NewsThread is for anyone who wants to understand the news, not just consume it. Whether you're a student, journalist, researcher, or curious citizen — see every side of every story.

Download NewsThread and break out of the echo chamber.
```
