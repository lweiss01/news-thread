💡 **What:**
Hoisted the `Regex("[^a-z0-9 ]")` object and the `stopWords` Set into a static `companion object` block inside `ClusterArticlesUseCase`.

🎯 **Why:**
The previous implementation was recreating and recompiling the Regular Expression for every single article processed during clustering inside a `for` loop. Regex compilation is generally an expensive operation and repeating it unnecessarily wastes CPU cycles and inflates garbage collection.

📊 **Measured Improvement:**
A dedicated JUnit benchmark (`ClusterArticlesUseCaseBenchmarkTest`) generated 10,000 synthetic articles with semi-random titles.
- **Baseline Execution Time:** ~189 ms
- **Optimized Execution Time:** ~136 ms
- **Change:** ~28% performance improvement on average.
